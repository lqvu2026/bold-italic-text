Bold Italic Text

Type your post, style the parts you want, then copy it into Facebook or LinkedIn.

Font:

Tap a style with words selected to style just those words. With nothing selected, it styles the whole text. You can also type **bold**, *italic* or ***both*** yourself.

Your styled text appears here.

Styled letters are special Unicode characters, not real formatting. They work on Facebook and LinkedIn, but screen readers may read them poorly and they can't be searched as normal text, so use them for a few key words rather than whole posts. Vietnamese works, but capitals that carry a mark (like Ô, Ư, Ạ) and the letters Đ and đ stay in normal type, because Unicode has no styled version that displays well.
